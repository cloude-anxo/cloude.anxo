## 🌱 About Cloude

Climate and environmental change can affect people not only through physical changes in their surroundings, but also through the way they think and feel about the future.

Cloude began with an observation that people can experience environmental or climate-related anxiety without necessarily recognizing what they are experiencing or understanding the environmental factors around them.

We wanted to explore a simple question:

> **Can technology help people better understand the environmental conditions around them and their relationship with those conditions?**

Cloude is our attempt to explore that question.

The broader project combines environmental information, historical context, and a user-focused digital experience to make environmental conditions easier to understand and more personally relevant.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Environmental information is widely available, but it is often presented through technical dashboards, raw measurements, or generalized forecasts.

At the same time, climate anxiety and environmental uncertainty are increasingly relevant topics, particularly among young people.

This creates a gap between:

**Environmental Data → Personal Understanding**

Cloude explores this gap by bringing environmental information closer to the individual and presenting it through a more accessible digital experience.

---

## 💡 The Idea

The main Cloude application includes an **"Environmental Signals Around Me"** experience.

The application can use the user's location to provide environmental and weather-related information relevant to their surroundings.

It also incorporates historical environmental information to provide additional context around current conditions.

The project uses environmental data provided through **Open-Meteo**.

The aim is not simply to display raw environmental measurements, but to help users understand what is happening around them and explore how environmental conditions may relate to their awareness and concerns.

## 🔬 Research Foundation — Project REACH

Cloude grew out of broader research and outreach work conducted through **Project REACH**, Anxothotl's flagship initiative.

Project REACH is a research-driven mental health outreach program that brings anxiety awareness and coping strategies to students across India.

Through workshops, surveys, and one-on-one interactions, the broader initiative has reached **40,000+ young individuals**.

The research and outreach work helped us understand how young people experience anxiety and how awareness can influence the way individuals interpret and respond to their experiences.

Cloude extends this research-oriented approach into the environmental space by exploring the relationship between environmental information and climate-related concerns.

---

## 🧠 From Research to Technology

The development of Cloude followed a simple progression:

**Research & Observation → Identify an Awareness Gap → Explore Environmental Signals → Design a Technology-Based Solution → Develop Cloude → Iterate & Improve**

The goal was not to build technology simply for the sake of technology.

The project began with an observed problem and explored how technology could provide one possible way of addressing it.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

The interface was designed around three ideas:

### 1. Simplicity

Environmental information can already be complicated.

The interface therefore aims to avoid unnecessary visual complexity.

### 2. Accessibility

The project is intended to make environmental concepts easier for users to approach and understand.

### 3. Continuity

The landing page, sign-in experience, and main application are designed as parts of one overall user journey rather than completely disconnected pages.
