Introduction

Climate intelligence, sensory relief

Cloude reads the air around you and syncs with what your body already knows, turning live environmental signals into gentle, practical guidance.

Read the environment

Live air, weather and environmental signals for where you are, grouped by how much they matter right now.

Sync with your body

Connect what is outside with how you feel inside, so triggers become easier to spot and plan around.

Built with Anxothotl

Cloude is powered by Anxothotl and Project REACH, with sensory relief at the centre of the design.
